# Third-party material

The nine layout selector illustrations in `assets/images/layout/` were copied from the installed Woodmart 8.5.7 theme by XTemos, whose theme header declares GNU GPL version 3.0. The source directories and original filenames are recorded in that folder's README. These illustrations are bundled locally; Woodmart is not required at runtime.

Device controls use WordPress Dashicons supplied by WordPress itself. Dashicons are not copied into this repository. Swiper is not included or required.

jsdom, PostCSS and jscpd are development-only dependencies, with versions and dependency licenses available through `package-lock.json` and their npm packages. They are excluded from release ZIPs.
